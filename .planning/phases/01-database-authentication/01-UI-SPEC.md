## Visuals & Focal Points

- **Focal Point:** Centered login card containing the matricule and password inputs on the screen.
- **Hierarchy:** Main focus is on the Accent-colored "Se connecter" CTA, followed by the form inputs.
- **Accessibility:** Text labels accompany all input fields.
